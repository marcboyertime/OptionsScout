# Decision policy

The six states are `ACTIONABLE`, `WATCH`, `NO_TRADE`, `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`, `INVALIDATED`, and `MARKET_CLOSED_OR_STALE`. Only ACTIONABLE passes every hard gate; WATCH preserves a time-bounded trigger; NO_TRADE is a substantive rejection; DATA_INSUFFICIENT is missing/unsafe input; INVALIDATED is a failed refreshed preflight; and MARKET_CLOSED_OR_STALE is a valid calendar/freshness block. One hard-gate failure blocks actionability; missing data is never a market-level no-trade conclusion.

Required gates include live synchronized tradable quotes, <=30 DTE, exact payoff, realistic natural fills, a source-backed sentence in the form “The market currently prices approximately X probability of Y outcome, and I believe this is wrong because Z”, underappreciation/evidence, distributional EV after costs/model error, event IV-crush, portfolio/correlation, mechanics, red-team evidence, contradictions, and price-change reanalysis.

All supplied typed structures are retained and the complete compatible chain also deterministically generates a bounded canonical comparison set. Same-horizon alternatives share the one sourced underlying spot/probability distribution: they cannot inject a favorable probability model. Each payoff is instead derived from its own conservative natural complex fill, bid/ask-derived entry/exit slippage, leg-count policy fee, and any separately reviewed costs. A no-riskier generated topology involving an unused chain contract, or any supplied alternative, participates in the policy-fee-aware `structure_selection` hard gate; a dominated selection cannot be ACTIONABLE. Before any non-selected alternative can compete, its legs must share exact symbol/expiration/multiplier/exercise/settlement mechanics. Any alternative short requires exact verified `index`/`index option` mechanics and all-European cash legs; a physical long alternative requires its own typed pre-expiry plan and conservative valuation rather than borrowing the selected structure's evidence. Required-before-expiry structures currently accept only sourced, exact-time `intrinsic_close_v1` valuation: scenario P/L is derived from the structure's signed intrinsic liquidation at typed spot and maximum permitted entry, with every cost subtracted; arbitrary payoff strings are rejected. This is a conservative lower bound only for pure long-option structures—any short leg fails closed until an independently validated short-extrinsic close model exists. Policy per-contract fees are included in all-in risk and cannot be offset by declared fees. For a spanning binary event, `event_iv_crush` values current signed complex value plus incremental implied-move-sized direction-correct/adverse delta, gamma, theta, and vega changes, then subtracts signed entry and all costs. Vega uses IV percentage points (`decimal_drop * 100`) and aggregate Greeks are multiplier-scaled exactly once. Near-zero delta fails closed because a direction-correct conservative case is undefined; positive actionability also needs a positive delayed/smaller-than-implied/severe-crush case.

The 50-gate ledger groups these checks into data/provenance, calendar/freshness, universe/equity, chain/contract/quote/liquidity, structure/payoff/economics, volatility/distribution, thesis/research/catalyst, portfolio/correlation, mechanics/containment, exit contingencies, skeptic/judge, and watch/preflight state. An actionable record requires a complete typed exit plan covering profit taking, underlying/thesis/volatility invalidation, time exit, catalyst handling, rapid gains, 50% loss, direction-versus-IV divergence, sector-versus-company divergence, expiration, assignment, and an explicit no-automatic-roll rule. A preflight also binds the exact selected order identity—topology/name, quantity, order type, structure-plan identity, leg count/set/order, side/ratio, and contract economic/mechanics terms—so a different valid trade always invalidates rather than reusing the decision. Outcomes are observations attached after a decision; they never rewrite decision-time thesis, gates, or recommendation.

Every report classifies `THEORETICAL_MAX_LOSS`, `OPERATIONAL_MAX_LOSS_RISK`, `ASSIGNMENT_RISK`, `PHYSICAL_SETTLEMENT_RISK`, and `POSSIBILITY_OF_ACCOUNT_DEFICIT`. Intended worst-case loss including premium, defined settlement, fees, commissions, and planned entry/exit slippage is capped at exactly $1,000. Normalized scenario payoff and sensitivity assertions are config-independent base economics: they include only declared structure-plan costs. Evaluation validates that base and then subtracts the configured per-contract policy fee exactly once from every scenario, sensitivity, EV, risk, and cap calculation. Negative costs and negative portfolio risks/limits are invalid. Exercise style is accepted only as exact `American` or `European`, and settlement only as exact lowercase `physical` or `cash`; unrecognized labels fail schema rather than falling through mechanics gates. Fill limits use signed whole-complex cash economics and require `realistic_entry <= target == declared fill limit <= maximum acceptable limit`, so neither debit nor credit structures receive a favorable unit mismatch. Total-loss probability is exact Decimal probability of P/L equal to the negative typed operational maximum loss, not the probability of the worst submitted scenario. Any required pre-expiry exit, including a cash-settled plan, stays `DATA_INSUFFICIENT` until a typed planned-exit valuation rather than an expiration distribution supports EV and sensitivity.

Scenario EV uses an exact typed expiration spot in the `bounded_terminal_spot_v1` model. Support is derived from underlying price, <=30 DTE, implied move, realized volatility, recorded historical event observations, and a 75% hard tail ceiling. Each supplied payoff must equal the selected structure's maximum-acceptable-fill, quantity/multiplier, fee, commission, and planned entry/exit-slippage economics; arbitrary scenario P/L is rejected. At least one `adverse_probability_shift_v1` sensitivity must move bounded probability toward the deterministic worst terminal (plus any declared non-negative extra cost), recompute exactly, and remain positive. Total-loss probability above 65% always fails; above 40% requires documented 3:1 terminal and 2x-risk asymmetry. Catalysts have a strict type and date. Historical events stay historical. An earnings, FDA, or macro catalyst within the selected expiration needs an exact matching upcoming event record, source/provenance, positive expected IV-drop assumption, and complete Greeks; the matrix uses that IV-drop, not the price implied move. A matching upcoming/spanning binary event also consumes the portfolio event-risk capacity.

American physically settled short legs are categorically rejected. More strictly, every short structure requires a verified index product and every leg must be European cash-settled; ETF/equity-labelled European/cash shorts remain ineligible. Long physical options require a pre-expiry close plan; no physical option is held through expiry. Liquidity is a strict policy-configured premium-band absolute/relative spread, OI, volume, underlying-liquidity, leg-count, and early-exit check, not a universal 20% spread rule.
